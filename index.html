About SmartAttend


SmartAttend is an advanced web-based attendance management system developed to simplify and modernize the attendance process in educational institutions. It uses Face Recognition, GPS Location Verification, and a Digital Attendance Dashboard to ensure secure and accurate attendance records. The system helps students, teachers, and administrators manage attendance efficiently through real-time updates, smart reports, AI Chatbot support, and easy-to-use dashboards. SmartAttend reduces manual work, improves accuracy, saves time, and provides a secure digital attendance experience.
